01 — About

How I think

Open a trait. Each one is a working habit, not a slogan — the way I actually approach a messy problem.

I started with data because I liked finding the structure inside noise. Then I kept running into the rest of the system: APIs, auth, databases, dashboards, the boring glue that makes a model useful. So I learned that too.

Now I move between software and data on purpose. I like products with real users, workflows that can be tightened, and tools I did not know last month. I am still growing — from beginner-intermediate toward someone who can own a system end to end — and I would rather show the work than the adjectives.

05 — Journey

From curious to shipping

A growth line, not a trophy shelf. No invented dates — just the sequence the work actually took.

  1. Learning

    Science and maths first. Higher secondary at PKD Matric, Pollachi — 91%. Then CIT, and the decision to treat data as a craft, not a subject.

  2. First projects

    Small systems that had to survive contact with users: a bilingual job portal, a sensor dashboard, messy notebooks that became products.

  3. Software development

    Frontend, backend, databases, APIs. The realization that a model without a working path around it is just a file.

  4. Real-world APIs

    Auth, payments, shipping, notifications. JWT, OTP, refresh tokens, FCM, Shiprocket — the unglamorous contracts between services.

  5. Marketplace systems

    Customers, vendors, catalogs, carts, checkout. Keeping state honest across a chain of people who all need different screens.

  6. Lab / service operations

    Admin, labs, services, customers, bookings. Software that has to match how a team actually runs a day.

  7. Odoo customization

    HR and payroll: employees, contracts, salary structures, payslips, QWeb reports. Making ERP match reality.

  8. Automation & AI

    Selenium, AutoEDA, NetraTax, air-quality forecasting. Where data science stops being coursework and starts deleting manual work.

  9. What’s next

    Deeper software engineering, cleaner automation, computer vision, IoT, and whatever problem is interesting enough to build.
